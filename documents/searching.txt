Keyword search ranks text by counting how often the words of a question
appear in it. A chunk that repeats a question word several times scores
higher than a chunk that mentions it once. This is crude but useful, and it
is the same idea behind the retrieval step of a search engine.
The obvious weakness is that keyword search only finds the exact words you
typed. A passage about a car will not match a question about an automobile,
because the two share no words at all.
